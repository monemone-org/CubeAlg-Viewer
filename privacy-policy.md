# Privacy Policy for CubeAlg Viewer

_Last updated: May 2026_

CubeAlg Viewer does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Data Storage

All data — including your algorithms, bookmarks, and solve history — is stored locally on your device. Nothing is transmitted to any server.

## In-App Purchases

CubeAlg Viewer offers optional in-app purchases processed by Apple via the App Store. We do not collect or store any payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs all payment transactions.

## Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.
