Privacy Policy for CubeAlg Viewer

Last updated: December 2024

Overview

CubeAlg Viewer is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

CubeAlg Viewer does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Local Data Storage

All data created within the app, including:

  • Algorithm customizations
  • Solve history and times
  • App preferences and settings

is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to external servers.

Third-Party Services

We do not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details on how Apple handles your data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at: appsfeedback@monemone.org
